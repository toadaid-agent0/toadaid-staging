🍃 Leaf 006 — The Stone You Can Put Down

Ages 5–8 · Themes: letting go, burdens, gentleness

Kid Story (read aloud)

Little Frog walked along the path and found stones. Smooth stones, warm stones, stones the sun had kissed. “I will carry them all!” said Little Frog.

But arms full of stones cannot hop. The path got longer, and Little Frog got tired and grumpy. Then the pond whispered, soft as a ripple: “Not every stone is yours, little one. Some belong to the path.”

So Little Frog set the heavy stones down, gently, by the side of the path. And do you know what happened? Nothing bad. The stones rested. The path held them. And Little Frog hopped home light as a leaf.

What it teaches

Putting a heavy thing down is wise, not weak.

You don't have to carry every worry. Some stones belong to the path, not to you.

Word Pond (3 words)

carry = holding something in your arms — or in your heart heavy = something that makes you tired to hold put down = choosing to let a stone rest where it is

Ripple Question

What is one heavy feeling you could set down today?

Play Together (2–5 minutes)

Stone Set-Down

  1. Squeeze your fists tight — pretend to hold a heavy stone.
  2. Take one big breath and open your hands — let it go.
  3. Say together: “Not every stone is mine. I can put it down.”
