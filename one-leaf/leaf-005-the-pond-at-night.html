🍃 Leaf 005 — The Pond at Night

Ages 5–8 · Themes: night, rest, gentleness

Kid Story (read aloud)

When the sun goes down, the pond gets quiet. The sky turns deep blue, like the pond’s blanket. Stars come out, one by one, like tiny lanterns.

The moon leans over the water to look at itself. The pond doesn’t hurry. It just holds the moon’s picture, soft and still, until morning.

Night is not scary on the pond. Night is the pond resting, getting ready for tomorrow. The pond whispered: “The dark is just the sky being gentle. Rest now. Tomorrow will come on its own.”

What it teaches

Rest is part of growing.

Even the pond rests. Night is how tomorrow gets ready.

Word Pond (3 words)

night = the sky’s quiet time rest = letting your body get ready for tomorrow gentle = soft and kind, never scary

Ripple Question

What do you like best about nighttime?

Play Together (2–5 minutes)

Star Count

  1. Look out the window before bed.
  2. Count 3 stars — or 3 quiet things you can hear.
  3. Say together: “The pond is resting. So am I.”
