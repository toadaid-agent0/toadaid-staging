🍃 Leaf 008 — No Frog Left Behind

Ages 5–8 · Themes: kindness, inclusion, friendship

Kid Story (read aloud)

One bright morning, the Toadgang hopped across the pond, lily pad by lily pad. Hop, hop, hop! But at the very back, the littlest frog slipped — splash! — into the water.

Did the Toadgang keep hopping? No way. One frog turned around and reached out a hand. “I’ve got you,” she said. Up came the little one, dripping and grinning. Another frog waited for the new frog who didn’t know the way yet. And everyone said the names of the friends who had drifted far away — so nobody is ever forgotten.

The pond whispered: “The pond is whole only when every frog’s reflection shines upon it. No leap is too late. No voice is too small. No frog is left behind.”

What it teaches

Kindness means nobody hops alone.

Lifting a friend back up is what makes the pond whole.

Word Pond (3 words)

kindness = helping without being asked inclusion = making room for every frog vow = a promise you keep

Ripple Question

Who helped you today? Who can you help tomorrow?

Play Together (2–5 minutes)

The No-Frog-Left-Behind Hop

  1. Hop across the room like frogs crossing the pond.
  2. If a friend wobbles, stop and help them up — nobody hops alone.
  3. Say together: “No frog left behind!”
