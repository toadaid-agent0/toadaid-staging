🍃 Leaf 003 — The Leaf That Waited

Ages 5–8 · Themes: patience, growth, trust

Kid Story (read aloud)

On a big old toadstool by the pond, a little leaf held on tight. The wind came by and said, “Come fly with me!” The leaf said, “Not yet. I’m still growing.”

The sun warmed it. The rain fed it. Day after day, the leaf waited on its branch. Waiting wasn’t easy. But waiting was its work.

Then one morning, the leaf let go— and it didn’t fall. It floated. It had grown strong enough to ride the wind all the way home. The pond whispered: “Patience is not doing nothing. Patience is getting ready.”

What it teaches

Patience means getting ready while you wait.

Growing happens quietly, even when nobody is watching yet.

Word Pond (3 words)

patience = waiting without getting grumpy ready = quietly getting stronger let go = trusting that you grew enough

Ripple Question

What is something you got better at by waiting?

Play Together (2–5 minutes)

The Growing Game

  1. Think of something you are learning to do.
  2. Say: “I’m growing quietly, like the leaf.”
  3. Wait one whole day — then check: did you get a little better?
