🍃 Leaf 004 — The First Ripple

Ages 5–8 · Themes: kindness, ripples, small acts

Kid Story (read aloud)

One quiet morning, a single drop of water fell into the pond. Plop. Just one small drop. Nobody even saw it fall.

But the pond felt it. A tiny ring spread out… then a bigger ring… then a bigger one. One small drop was saying hello to the whole pond.

That is how kindness works too. One kind word can ripple all the way across. The pond whispered: “Small things travel far. Your kindness is never just yours.”

What it teaches

Kindness spreads like ripples — further than you can see.

You may never know how far your small kindness traveled.

Word Pond (3 words)

ripple = a ring that travels on water kindness = making someone’s day softer spread = going further and further out

Ripple Question

What small kind thing could you do today that might ripple?

Play Together (2–5 minutes)

The Ripple Clap

  1. One person claps a slow rhythm.
  2. Everyone joins in one by one, like rings spreading.
  3. Say together: “Small things travel far.”
